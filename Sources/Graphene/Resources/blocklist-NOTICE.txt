Conversion: scripts/make-blocklist.py in the Graphene source distribution.
The generated subset keeps 2,000 source-backed domain anchors. It prioritizes
common ad networks and samples the remaining source deterministically. Rules
are restricted to third-party network requests. Cosmetic filters, resource-type
conditions and site-specific exception logic are not a complete EasyList
implementation. Disable blocking per site if a page breaks. No runtime list
updates, request counts, or network telemetry are performed by Graphene.
