What changes from the current build

  • Flat tinted sidebar becomes a flooded two-stop gradient with grain over the whole window. Dark spaces go deep and saturated instead of grey.
  • The address pill leaves the sidebar. Navigation and the URL move into a 32pt toolbar on the page card. Clicking the URL opens the command bar.
  • The single oversized favorite tile becomes a grid of equal 44pt tiles, three per row.
  • The "Research ···" header shrinks to an 11pt space label. "Tidy | Clear" becomes a hover-only "Clear" on the Today hairline. "Ask Graphene ⌘K" leaves the footer; Ask lives on the toolbar.
  • Threads, Vault, Mail and Board leave the sidebar. They open from the command bar, the footer library glyph and ⌘⌥1–4.
  • The page card gets an 8pt gap, a 1px hairline and a soft shadow instead of a thick tinted border.
  • Command bar loses its chips and footer legend and gains Arc's geometry.
  • Sidebar collapse, Little Arc and the chat panel move with springs. Reduce Motion turns everything into fades.
