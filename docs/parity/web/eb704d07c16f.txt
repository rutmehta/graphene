This article applies to Arc on
macOS
and Windows, but not Arc for
iOS
or Arc Search.
Split View in Arc Browser supercharges your multitasking by allowing you to view and interact with multiple tabs simultaneously in one window. When you create a split view, it becomes its own new tab in the sidebar that you can come back to later. This includes:
Horizontal Split View
, or viewing tabs side-by-side
Vertical Split View
, or viewing tabs top-and-bottom
This feature is perfect for users who need to compare information across different sites or want to keep essential content readily visible while browsing.
Shortcuts
MacOS:
Command-Shift-Plus,
or drag and drop a tab from the sidebar into the center of the screen
Windows:
Control-Shift-Plus
Or, use
Command-T
(MacOS) or
Control-T
(Windows) then type "Add Right Split," "Add Left Split," "Add Top Split," or "Add Bottom Split" to orient the location of your Split View.
Exit Split View
Right-click on a Split Tab and select "Separate All Tabs"
Hit the X above either split view panel
Hit the X next to either Split View Tab in the Sidebar
Swap out Split View Panels
To change the URL of a split panel, just
hit Command-L
on macOS or Control-L on Windows.
You can also edit the address bar on your current panel.
