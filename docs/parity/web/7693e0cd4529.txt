Hello from Manhattan, NY
Every company's kit looks different. At BCNY, ours is mostly Slack and GSuite (and now Atlassian!). That's where decisions get made, insights are shared, and plans are developed. But at my first job, it was Microsoft all the way.
With Dia's new suite of Microsoft tools, that same power opens up for a lot more teams. A couple of things that now just work: a heads-up in your morning brief when a doc updates in SharePoint and asking Dia what's happening in Teams.
