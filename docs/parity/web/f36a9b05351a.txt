Hello from Brooklyn, NY
Lately I’ve been using
Dia’s Linear integration
to stay on top of everything the Tools team is shipping and what needs my attention.
My go to prompt at the top of the week is: “Visualize a table summarizing the status, health, and progress of every project in Linear on the Tools team this week, sorted by risk, and flag any whose status doesn't match their actual issue activity.”
This brings to the forefront everything I need to check up on, and gives me a great overview of where the team is at.
