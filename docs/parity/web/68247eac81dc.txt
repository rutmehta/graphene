Do students get early access to the beta?
Yes! Sign up with your academic email for early access. We identify students according to the WHED.
Are there any discounts for students?
Not yet! We don’t have a student plan at the moment, but we know how much it matters. It’s something we’d love to offer down the road, so stay tuned.
What devices are currently supported?
Right now, Dia runs on Apple macOS 14+ with M1 chips or later.
Is The Browser Company hiring interns?
We don’t have a formal internship program at The Browser Company. That being said, if you’re creating content and experimenting with new ideas, we’d be excited to chat!
