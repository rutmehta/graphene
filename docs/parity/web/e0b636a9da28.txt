What are Favorites?
Favorites
are easy to access tabs that that live across all your Spaces. When you Favorite a tab, it's icon shows at the very top of the Sidebar, above your Space Titles. You can have up to 12 Favorites in your Sidebar.
What's the difference between Favorites and Pinned Tabs?
Favorites are Pinned Tabs that are accessible in every Space. Learn more about Pinned Tabs
here
!
How to Favorite a Tab
Drag a tab above your Space title, to the very top of the sidebar
Type Command-T (macOS) or Control-T (Windows) then Move To Favorite Tab
Right Click Tab > Move To > Favorites
How to Un-Favorite a Tab
Drag a tab to the Pinned Section or Un-Pinned Sections below the Space Title.
Type
Command-T
(macOS) or
Control-T
(Windows) then
Move To [
Sidebar Section
]
Right Click Tab > Move To > [
Sidebar Section
]
