This article applies to Arc for
macOS
, but not Arc for
Windows
.
Arc streamlines your note-taking process by allowing you to set a default notes application that best suits your workflow. Now that
Arc Notes has been phased out
, we strongly encourage you to change your default notes app, which will allow you to continue creating notes in
Notion
,
Google Docs
,
Word
or
Confluence
.
Change Your Default Notes App
Instead of using Arc Notes, you can use another note-taking service via the Command Bar.
Go to
Arc
>
Settings
>
Profiles
and use the dropdown under "
New Documents
" to select another platform as your default notes app.
Use
Command+T
and type “
New Note
” to begin taking notes on the platform you selected.
