Greetings from Kansas City
For those who have been waiting, they're finally here. For those who are newer to Dia,
context switching just got a lot simpler
.
Profiles keep your logins, your tools, your AI, your context, exactly where they belong. The missing piece was getting between them quickly. So we added a better way to context switch.
To try it:
right-click on the tab bar, create a new Profile, and swipe away.
P.S. Curious how we landed here?
Check out
the roundtable discussion
with the design minds behind the swipe.
